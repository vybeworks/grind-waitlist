What's actually inside

Weekly Rhythm

Never stare at a blank screen again

Set your posting and music-making rhythm once, in your own words — Monday's your studio day, Thursday's release prep. It just runs, every week, so you're never sitting there at 11pm wondering what to post.

Auto Tracking

Stop screenshotting your own stats

Connect Instagram and YouTube once. GRIND pulls your real views, likes, and follows automatically — no more scrolling back through your own analytics trying to remember how last week's post did.

Release Toolkit

Your streak, your consistency, your stats, your release activity — combined into a single score that reflects real momentum. Not XP for the sake of feeling good.

Insight Engine

Before you go

Questions people actually ask

Is GRIND free?+

Core features — Weekly Rhythm, tracking, and streaks — will have a free tier. Deeper features like automatic sync and the Insight Engine are part of a paid plan once GRIND launches.

When does it launch?+

GRIND is actively being built right now. Waitlist members get notified the moment it's live — before anyone else.

What platforms will it support?+

Instagram and YouTube sync automatically. TikTok is supported through quick manual logging, since TikTok doesn't offer the same public API access.

Do I need a big following to use it?+

No — GRIND is built for artists at any stage, especially the ones just starting to take their content and career seriously.
